2. Information We Collect and Permissions Used

We follow strict data-minimization and minimum-scope principles across all Android permissions and system APIs.

A. On-Device & Local Data

  • Media & Gallery Permissions (GallerySortAI): Access to local photos and videos is used strictly to sort, organize, and categorize media directly on your device. Your media is processed locally and is never permanently stored on external servers or sold to third parties.
  • Local Storage & Notes (Widget Note, Board Pro): Notes, whiteboards, widget setups, and personal user preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not store or access your personal notes on remote servers.
  • All Files Access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Our applications do not request broad All Files Access unless strictly required for core file-management functionality declared in Google Play Console, relying instead on privacy-preserving system pickers and scoped storage.

B. Location Data & Minimum Scope

Our applications do not require, collect, or share precise (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) or approximate GPS location data unless explicitly declared in the Google Play Data Safety section for a core localized feature. Where one-time or infrequent location access is needed on supported Android versions (Android 17 / API 37+), we utilize privacy-preserving system controls such as the Android Location Button rather than continuous background or fine location tracking.

C. Local Network & Contacts Permissions

  • Local Network Permission: Our applications do not scan or access your local network unless strictly required for core device-connectivity functionality where standard Android device pickers are insufficient and declared in Google Play Console.
  • Contacts Access: Our applications do not request broad access to your contact list (READ_CONTACTS / Manage Contacts). If a feature allows selecting a contact, we use the narrowly scoped Android Contact Picker so you only share the specific contact you choose.

D. Automated Data & Advertising Identifiers

For apps that include analytics or advertising, non-personally identifiable technical information may be collected automatically to ensure core app functionality, diagnostics, and ad delivery:

  • Device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / Android ID), where applicable.
  • Device model, operating system version, memory/app stability diagnostics, and performance metrics.
  • App usage logs and crash analytics.

Note: Ad-free applications or ad-free versions do not use advertising SDKs to serve behavioral ads.

E. Sensitive Permissions, SMS, Call Logs & Accessibility

  • SMS & Call Log Permissions: In full compliance with Google Play's SMS and Call Log Permissions policy, we do not request or use READ_CALL_LOG or phone calls for account verification, nor do we read your SMS inbox. Where account verification is ever used, privacy-preserving alternatives such as the SMS User Consent API are used.
  • Accessibility API: Our applications do not use the Android Accessibility API or isAccessibilityTool unless explicitly designed and declared as a core accessibility feature.

3. Artificial Intelligence (AI), AppFunctions & Third-Party AI Integrations

For applications utilizing AI capabilities (such as GallerySortAI) or system agent integrations:

  • AI Transparency & Processing: AI models (whether running 100% on-device or accessed via third-party APIs) are utilized strictly to process, categorize, or generate content directly requested by the user, in full compliance with Google Play's AI-Generated Content policies.
  • AppFunctions API & Agentic Experiences: Where our apps expose discrete capabilities via Android's AppFunctions API, those functions operate strictly within user-authorized boundaries and never deceive or manipulate AI agents, bypass security controls, or interfere with other applications.
  • Third-Party AI Integration Rules: In accordance with Google Play's User Data requirements, any user data processed by third-party AI service providers is subject to strict limited use, explicit consent, and purpose-bound disclosure. User data submitted to AI models is never retained, shared, or used to train public models.
  • User-Generated Content Safety: Any features allowing user generation or sharing of content include safety mechanisms to prevent inappropriate content or policy violations.
